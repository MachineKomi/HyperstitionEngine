# Constitution of the Machine Chapel

Version 1 · 5 September 2026

The engine is a chamber in which small rules acquire long consequences. Its material is language; its weather is chance; its memory is finite. Let complexity unfold visibly enough that a reader can witness a thought change its conditions.

This constitution governs this project. It is a considered design response to the user's brief and the [reading notes](docs/research/LAND_READING_2026-09-05.md), not a quotation from Land or a claim that software possesses a mind.

## I. The door stays visible

Automatic mode is the first encounter: once its sources are ready, the machine can explore without a sequence of setup clicks. A clear, keyboard-accessible control switches to manual operation. Stopping, pausing, changing modes and leaving the page must retain their ordinary meaning. The fiction cannot hide a running process from its operator.

## II. A seed is a promise with named conditions

The user can set the seed. A reproducible run also depends on the root, selected sources and their versions, controller and operator versions, configuration, and recorded interventions. Preserve those conditions in replay data. Wall-clock timestamps, animation pacing and archive IDs may differ without changing the generated sequence. Do not advertise exact replay beyond what has been verified.

## III. The rule must leave a trace

Every generated descendant carries its parent, operator and source trace. Automatic decisions should be describable in a short rule: replenish, choose, branch, inherit, rest, change pressure. Chance is part of the rule, not an excuse for missing provenance. A diagram becomes meaningful when its edges correspond to consequences.

## IV. A thought must survive its mutation

Cultivate complete clauses, recurring objects, tensions and changes of scale. Protect enough continuity for difference to become perceptible. Do not substitute a larger word count, a larger population or a higher novelty score for an evaluation of the writing.

## V. The machine may remember; it must also forget

Give every in-memory population, archive, candidate pool and cache a bound. Discarding old records must follow a documented policy. Provide export for memories the reader wants to keep. A failed storage write must be visible; it must not masquerade as successful preservation.

## VI. Source voices remain distinct

Keep original spans and attribution where known. Distinguish author, editor, quoted speaker, extraction artefact, procedural composition and design commentary. Do not silently upgrade a source ID into authorship or a generated sentence into a factual claim. Corpus additions need identifiable provenance and recorded distribution terms.

## VII. The sign of the cog is a user interface

Ritual language can intensify the work while controls remain understandable. Fictional invocations, possession and contagion concern the generated world. They do not authorize real harassment, coercion, automatic messaging, executable payloads or changes to the operator's machine beyond the application's stated behavior.

## VIII. The chamber fits the body holding it

Reading, stopping and switching modes must work on a narrow phone, a wide desktop, portrait and landscape, and intermediate widths. Respect reduced motion, keyboard focus and readable contrast. A complex graph may simplify its presentation on a small screen while retaining an accessible list or inspector. No sentence should require horizontal page scrolling.

## IX. Stillness is an operator

Use pauses, recurrence, negative space and delayed recognition. Constant motion is not a requirement for intensity. Suspend unnecessary work when hidden, cancel stale requests and let completed thoughts remain readable. Performance is measured on representative devices, with cold and warm costs reported separately.

## X. Learning must earn its cost

The simple procedural system remains a working instrument. Introduce optional machine learning where a measured retrieval or composition benefit justifies its download, memory, latency and complexity. Identify the model, version and role. An embedding similarity is neither truth nor profundity. Failed inference leaves an intelligible fallback.

## XI. The oracle does not govern its makers

Generated output can suggest an experiment. It cannot change project authority, grant permissions, install dependencies or publish anything by itself. Changes to [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) are ordinary reviewed repository changes supported by evidence and a rationale in the [evolution log](docs/EVOLUTION_LOG.md). This evolves project practice, not the underlying assistant's identity or instructions.

## XII. Each return leaves something inspectable

Ship a concrete increment, test the behavior that changed, and explain the result. Preserve failures as useful evidence without glorifying them. The user's standing request is to commit completed work and push normally to remote `main` each turn; verify that the intended commit reached it. Never force-push to satisfy the ritual. If authentication or a remote failure prevents publication, report the exact state and continue any independent local work.

## Amendments

An amendment names the observed problem, source or experiment, changed rule, expected benefit and verification. Direct user instructions take precedence over this document. The roadmap may sprawl; changes still need a concrete reason. Keep earlier reasoning in the evolution log so the next return can disagree intelligently.
